Choosing between the Amazon Echo (4th Gen) and Google Nest Audio largely comes down to the ecosystem you already use. Both speakers deliver strong room-filling sound and reliable voice control, but they take different approaches to smart-home integration.

## Quick take

Choose the Echo if you want a built-in smart-home hub, broad Alexa compatibility, and strong value. Choose the Nest Audio if you prefer Google Assistant, Google services, and multi-room listening across a Google-powered home.

## The deciding difference

The Echo is the more flexible starting point for a new smart home because its built-in Zigbee hub can connect compatible devices directly. Nest Audio is the smoother fit for households already invested in Google Assistant, Android, YouTube, or Google Home.
