Google Nest Audio and the Philips Hue Starter Kit improve very different parts of the home. Nest Audio adds room-filling sound and Google Assistant, while Hue transforms the atmosphere and automation of your lighting.

## Quick take

Choose Nest Audio for music, podcasts, voice queries, and Google Home control. Choose Philips Hue for richer lighting scenes, dependable scheduling, and a more visible change to the feel of a room.

## Which should come first?

If your home already has decent lighting but lacks a useful voice assistant, Nest Audio is the more practical first step. If you already have a speaker and want a more immersive environment, Hue is the upgrade you will notice throughout the day.
