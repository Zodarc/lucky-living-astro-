Philips Hue and the Amazon Echo are often considered together because they complement each other, but they are not interchangeable products. Hue is a lighting platform; Echo is a voice assistant and smart-home controller.

## Quick take

Start with Philips Hue when the priority is reliable, highly customizable lighting. Start with Echo when you want voice control, routines, timers, and a central hub for a broader range of smart-home devices.

## The best pairing

For many homes, the strongest answer is both: use Hue for lighting and Echo for voice control and automation. The Echo's Zigbee hub can connect compatible Hue devices without requiring a separate bridge, although the Hue Bridge unlocks the full Hue feature set.
