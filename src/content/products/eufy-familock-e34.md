# eufy FamiLock E34 Review

The eufy FamiLock E34 is a premium smart lock with an unusual idea at its center: instead of relying primarily on a fingerprint, phone, or NFC card, it uses **palm-vein recognition** for contactless entry.

That makes the E34 one of the more distinctive smart locks available, but innovation alone does not justify a $279.99 list price.

The bigger question is whether palm-vein unlocking, built-in Wi-Fi, Matter support, and the E34's dual-battery system provide enough everyday value to justify the premium.

> **Spoky Curates testing note:** This is an evidence-based review based on manufacturer documentation and independent user feedback. We have not physically tested the E34, so manufacturer claims are not presented as our own hands-on test results.

## Quick Verdict

The eufy FamiLock E34 is a **very good smart lock for buyers who specifically want contactless palm-vein access**.

Its strongest features are the palm scanner, built-in Wi-Fi, Matter over Thread support, and substantial battery system.

However, the E34 has some important limitations.

It does not support Apple Home Key, third-party Matter ecosystems require compatible controller hardware, and the lock does not have a door-position sensor.

For Apple Home users, the Aqara U200 deserves serious consideration.

## Palm-Vein Unlocking

Palm-vein recognition is the E34's defining feature.

According to eufy, the lock can recognize a registered palm in approximately 0.6 seconds and supports up to 50 registered palm-vein users.

The appeal is obvious. You can unlock the door without:

- pulling out your phone
- remembering a PIN
- touching a fingerprint reader
- carrying a key

This could be particularly convenient for families.

However, eufy's speed and recognition figures are manufacturer claims. Independent owner experiences are not completely uniform, so buyers should not interpret the advertised accuracy as a guarantee of identical real-world performance.

## Battery Life

The E34 uses a 10,000mAh rechargeable main battery.

eufy claims up to eight months of operation, although actual battery life will depend on usage, wireless connectivity, environmental conditions, and other factors.

The lock also includes a 750mAh backup battery.

That dual-battery arrangement is one of the E34's most attractive practical features because it provides another layer of protection against a depleted primary battery.

There is also USB-C emergency power.

## Built-In Wi-Fi

The E34 includes built-in Wi-Fi.

That means users can remotely control the lock through eufy's own ecosystem without buying a separate Wi-Fi bridge specifically for the lock.

This is an advantage over smart locks where remote access requires another device.

Matter is slightly different.

For third-party Matter ecosystems, a compatible Matter controller is still required.

## Matter Support

The E34 supports Matter over Thread.

eufy lists compatibility with:

- Apple Home
- Google Home
- Amazon Alexa
- Samsung SmartThings

However, there is a very important distinction for Apple users.

### The E34 does not support Apple Home Key.

That means an iPhone or Apple Watch cannot be used for the tap-to-unlock experience associated with Apple Home Key.

The E34 can participate in Apple Home through Matter, but that is not the same feature.

## Installation

The E34 is designed for compatible standard doors and is intended to replace the existing deadbolt.

eufy lists compatibility around 35–55mm door thickness.

Before purchasing, check the existing door and deadbolt configuration carefully.

Smart locks are not universally compatible, and a lock that works perfectly on one door may not fit another.

## Security

eufy lists the E34 as ANSI/BHMA Grade 1 and IP65 rated.

The company also states that palm-vein information is stored locally.

These specifications are useful, but smart-lock security has multiple layers.

Mechanical strength, correct installation, account security, software updates, wireless security, and access management all matter.

## Auto-Lock Limitation

One of the E34's less obvious weaknesses is the lack of a door-position sensor.

The lock can automatically lock after a configured period, but it does not independently determine whether the door is physically closed.

That distinction matters.

A timed auto-lock is not equivalent to a lock that knows the door has actually closed.

## What We Like

### Contactless unlocking

Palm-vein recognition is genuinely different and could be extremely convenient for households that frequently enter with full hands.

### Strong power redundancy

The 10,000mAh primary battery combined with the 750mAh backup battery is reassuring.

### Matter support

Matter over Thread gives the lock a route into several major smart-home platforms.

### Built-in Wi-Fi

Remote control through eufy's ecosystem does not require a separate Wi-Fi bridge.

### No mandatory subscription

The core lock experience does not depend on a monthly subscription.

## What We Don't Like

### No Apple Home Key

This is the biggest ecosystem limitation.

### Expensive

At $279.99 list price, the E34 is competing against some very capable alternatives.

### No door-position sensor

The absence of door-state detection makes its auto-lock functionality less sophisticated than some competing products.

### Mixed independent feedback

Owner experiences with battery performance and software are not completely consistent.

## Who Should Buy the E34?

The E34 makes the most sense for:

- Families wanting contactless entry
- Buyers interested in palm-vein technology
- Existing eufy users
- Smart-home enthusiasts using Matter
- People who value battery redundancy

## Who Should Skip It?

Consider another lock if:

- Apple Home Key is essential
- You want the lowest-cost Matter lock
- You need a retrofit installation
- Door-position detection is important
- You do not care about palm-vein recognition

## Final Verdict

The eufy FamiLock E34 is an **innovative premium smart lock**, but it is not automatically the best smart lock for everyone.

Palm-vein recognition is its biggest advantage, while the dual-battery system and built-in Wi-Fi make the hardware proposition stronger.

But the $279.99 list price is difficult to ignore.

The lack of Apple Home Key and door-position sensing also matter.

**Spoky Curates Rating: 4.1/5**

If palm-vein unlocking is the feature you have been looking for, the E34 is one of the most interesting choices available.

If you simply want the most versatile smart lock for the money, the Aqara U200 is worth comparing before you buy.
